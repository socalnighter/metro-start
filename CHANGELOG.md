# Changelog

## 1.15.0
- New: make a folder by dragging one tile onto another. Hold it over the middle of the other tile for half a second until the outline says "Make a folder", then let go. The folder is named after both tiles ("Gmail + Drive"), takes the other tile's spot, size and color, and nothing else moves. Undo splits them again. Works inside folders too (it makes a subfolder). A quick drop, or a drop near a tile's edge, still just moves the tile.

## 1.14.1
- Start now uses as many rows as fit on your screen (up to 12) instead of always 6, so a large tile can go under another large tile. Tiles you've already placed keep their spots.
- Fixed a harmless error when saving the tile editor right after typing an address.

## 1.14.0
- New: news feed tiles. Add one from **+ → Add a news feed** (BBC News, NPR, The New York Times, The Guardian, The Verge, Ars Technica, Google News), or paste any site's address and Metro Start finds its feed. Large, wide and medium tiles show the newest story's photo and headline; small tiles show the site's icon and a count of new stories. Tiles don't flip or rotate; they update quietly every 20 minutes and keep the last stories for when you're offline. Right-click → **All headlines** shows the latest stories with thumbnails.
- New: more clocks. Scroll over the clock (or use the arrow keys) to switch between your own time and other cities. Starts with Los Angeles, New York, UTC and New Delhi (IST); add any city or remove these in Personalize. A clock in your own time zone is skipped.
- New: more weather places. Add cities in Personalize, then scroll over the weather to switch between them and your location.

## 1.13.0
- The right-click menu is now short, with submenus. "Move to folder" opens a list of your folders, and folders that have subfolders open their own submenu, so Delete is always on screen. Submenus open on hover or click, and work with the arrow keys (→ opens, ← goes back, Esc closes).
- "Add a game" in the + menu is a submenu too.
- New default tiles (for new installs, or Personalize → Reset): Google sites are grouped in a Google folder, and streaming and music services (Netflix, YouTube, Hulu, Max, Disney+, Prime Video, Apple TV+, Spotify, Twitch) are in an Entertainment folder.

## 1.12.3
- Fixed: tiles inside folders were stacked in one narrow column. Folders now use the full width of the window.
- Fixed: tiles inside folders snapped back after being moved or resized. Like the Start screen, a folder now keeps each tile on the square you put it on, shows an outline while dragging, and moves tiles that are in the way to the nearest free space.

## 1.12.2
- Fixed: a tile dropped in the empty space beside or below your tiles snapped back. The whole Start area now accepts drops, and dragging near the left or right edge scrolls.
- Fewer entries on the extension's Errors page: weather problems are logged as information, and bookmark-import icon lookups no longer follow redirects to other sites (which Chrome reported as errors).

## 1.12.1
- Fixed: tiles snapped back after being moved or resized. Tiles now keep the exact square you put them on. While dragging, an outline shows where the tile will land, and any tile in the way moves to the nearest free space. Resizing keeps the tile's corner in place.
- Fixed: making several changes quickly could briefly bring back an older layout.

## 1.12.0
- New tile design: the site's icon at the top left, its name in bold beside it, and its address underneath.
- Tiles always appear in your order: they flow left to right and top to bottom in groups, like Windows 8, instead of jumping ahead to fill gaps.
- Bookmark import keeps your bookmarks' order, and bookmarks with long names get double-width tiles (can be turned off).
- Erase-and-import keeps your games by default. New "All games" option (and a button in Personalize) brings the Games folder back or fills in missing games.
- New games: Cat Nap, a cat logic puzzle with a daily puzzle, and Pairs, an animal memory match.
